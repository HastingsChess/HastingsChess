# Implemented rule decisions

- Charge draws are once per eligible White turn and conditional on no previous charge. Move 30 is certain if reached. Checkmate/stalemate before White starts takes precedence.
- Original White pawn/housecarl identities are processed by file a–h, then descending rank. A pushed original unit keeps its identity and can act later; no unit duplicates. The charge expires en passant. A king is neither pushed nor captured.
- A housecarl tries up to two forward squares. It captures only an enemy directly ahead. If the destination two away is occupied and cannot be pushed, it stops in front, even when that piece is an enemy. Ordinary housecarl moves and captures are one square in any direction.
- A pawn tries three forward squares. At an occupied square it first tries pushing the blocker one square forward. If that cannot be done and the blocker is an enemy, it captures it. Friendly blockers cannot be captured. A unit action is atomic for the king-safety check; no pieces disappear or overlap. Accepted earlier units remain accepted.
- A compulsory White unit movement exposing White's own king to check still happens unless the resulting position is immediate White checkmate under ordinary legal replies. A White pawn reaching rank eight in the charge becomes a housecarl. Ordinary promotions for both sides are standard chess.
- After the charge, Black has one normal action and two bonus actions. Bonus actions are legal knight actions or a pawn single advance, diagonal capture or direct forward capture. Bonus pawns cannot make double moves, en passant or promotions; a pawn on rank two has no bonus move to rank one. King safety applies to each action. A mating action ends the game; if none is legal, a bonus slot is skipped.
- If Black has no legal ordinary response after the charge but can resolve check using a legal bonus action, that bonus is performed first and consumes one slot; then Black has the ordinary action and remaining bonus. If neither ordinary nor rescue bonus exists, normal checkmate/stalemate detection applies.
- Replays are immutable event snapshots. Versions 1 and 2 remain viewable as historical chronicles, not revalidated under later rules. Version 3 records the bonus ruleset and exceptional phase order. Repetition and insufficient material are not adjudicated; the fifty-move rule is supported.
