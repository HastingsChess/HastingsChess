# Rule decisions

- The turn-20–30 percentages are conditional on reaching that White turn without a charge. The RNG is drawn once on that eligible turn. If White is already checkmated or stalemated, the game has ended and no draw occurs.
- A charge processes original White pawn and housecarl identities by file a–h, then descending rank within each file. A pushed original unit retains its identity and may act later. A charge is one compulsory turn, so en passant expires.
- A proposed unit action is evaluated after its entire advance, push/capture and any promotion. It is skipped if at that point White is in check and has zero ordinary legal White moves. Earlier accepted unit actions remain. This is the operational meaning of “immediately checkmated”; ordinary check alone does not exempt an action. An already checked king can be rescued by later units if the current action does not produce checkmate.
- A stationary unit blocked by a king or friendly piece records the obstruction but performs no action. No king may be pushed or captured. A blocker on a rook's original square that moves or is captured revokes that side's relevant castling right.
- The Norman ordinary response must be legal. If it gives checkmate, the bonus sequence ends. Otherwise each bonus action is a fresh legal knight move, including pins and check restrictions. A missing legal knight action is skipped; the second is checked separately. Stalemate is evaluated when normal alternating turns resume, since Black is still entitled to its bonus actions. A Norman king check after the charge must be answered on the ordinary response.
- The fifty-move draw rule is supported. Repetition and insufficient-material adjudication are not implemented. JSON replays contain every special action, because standard PGN cannot encode this turn structure.
